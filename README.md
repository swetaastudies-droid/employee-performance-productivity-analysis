# Employee Performance & Productivity Analysis

## 📌 Project Overview

This project focuses on analyzing employee performance and productivity using **Power BI and Microsoft Excel**. The analysis provides HR-focused insights into employee performance, productivity, utilization, training, and target achievement.

## 🎯 Objectives

- Analyze overall employee performance and productivity.
- Evaluate employee utilization and target achievement.
- Examine the relationship between training and productivity.
- Identify high-performing employees.
- Compare performance across departments.
- Perform what-if scenario analysis to understand potential improvements.

## 🛠️ Tools & Technologies

- Microsoft Power BI
- Microsoft Excel
- Data Analysis & Visualization
- HR Analytics
- What-If Scenario Analysis

## 📊 Key KPIs

- Average Performance Rating
- Average Utilization %
- High Performer %
- Average Training Hours
- Target Achievement %

## 🔍 Key Analysis Areas

### 1. Employee Performance
Analysis of employee performance ratings and performance categories.

### 2. Productivity & Utilization
Evaluation of employee productivity and utilization levels across the workforce.

### 3. Department-Wise Analysis
Comparison of employee performance and productivity across different departments.

### 4. Training Impact
Analysis of training hours and their relationship with employee productivity and performance.

### 5. Star Performer Analysis
Identification of high-performing employees based on performance and utilization indicators.

### 6. What-If Scenario Analysis
Interactive scenarios were developed in Power BI to understand the potential impact of changes in training and target achievement.

## 💡 HR Recommendations

- Strengthen targeted employee training programs.
- Identify development needs among lower-performing employees.
- Balance workloads to improve employee utilization.
- Encourage mentoring and knowledge sharing.
- Monitor performance and productivity KPIs regularly.
